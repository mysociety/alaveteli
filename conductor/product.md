# Initial Concept
An open source project to create a standard, internationalised platform for making Freedom of Information (FOI) requests in different countries around the world.

# Product Guide - Alaveteli

## 1. Vision & Core Mission
Alaveteli is a standard, internationalised open-source platform designed to lower the barrier of entry for citizens making Freedom of Information (FOI) requests to public authorities. By publishing requests and responses online automatically, it creates a searchable public archive of government information, fostering transparency, accountability, and citizen engagement.

## 2. Core User Personas
*   **Public Citizens:** Individuals who want to ask public bodies for information. They need an extremely simple, jargon-free interface that guides them through the request process.
*   **Journalists & Researchers (Pro Users):** Power users who need to make batch requests to multiple public bodies, embargo responses until publication dates, and track request metrics.
*   **Site Administrators / Moderators:** Trusted volunteers or staff who manage the public body database, moderate inappropriate requests or responses, and handle user support.
*   **Public Bodies:** Civil servants and government officers who receive the requests. They require clear, structured emails and a simple way to respond.

## 3. Key Feature Areas
*   **Request & Response Flow:** A guided multi-step form for writing requests, with automated delivery via email, and a parser to route incoming email replies back to the public request page.
*   **Public Directory of Authorities:** A database of public bodies (categorized by type/jurisdiction) with contact details, statistics on response rates, and average response times.
*   **Search & Archive:** A highly searchable public archive powered by Xapian, enabling users to find previous requests, documents, and public bodies.
*   **Alaveteli Pro (Premium Tools):** A suite of paid or restricted features including private requests (embargoes), batch requests, dashboard metrics, and advanced searches.
*   **Moderation & Admin Suite:** Administrative interface for hidden/removed requests, banning users, editing public bodies, and managing spam.
*   **Internationalisation & Localisation:** Robust support for multiple languages and country-specific FOI laws/customizations.

## 4. Key Success Metrics
*   **Request Volume:** Number of active requests successfully sent and tracked.
*   **Public Body Response Rate:** Percentage of requests resolved or answered.
*   **User Retention:** Active users returning to read, follow, or create new requests.
*   **Search Discovery:** Traffic originating from search engines discovering archived public data.
