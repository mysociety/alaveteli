# Alaveteli

Software that lets people make Freedom of Information requests to public authorities and publishes the correspondence.

## Language

**Announcement**:
A message from the site's admins shown to people, either to everyone or to people with a particular role. Each person can dismiss it.
_Avoid_: Banner, alert, notice

**Visibility**:
Who an Announcement is shown to: everyone, or people holding a role such as admin or pro.
_Avoid_: Audience, targeting

**Dismissal**:
A record that a person has closed an Announcement, so it is hidden from them and any older Announcement.
_Avoid_: Acknowledgement

**Note**:
A message from the site's admins attached to a record such as an authority, request or category, or to every record carrying a tag, shown alongside it.
_Avoid_: Announcement, comment

**Style**:
The colour treatment of an Announcement or Note: red, yellow, green or blue. An Announcement also has a default Style that keeps its standard look; a Note has a deprecated original Style for plain text.
_Avoid_: Type, theme, colour scheme

**Rich text**:
Formatted content written in the admin editor, used for the body of Announcements and Notes in every locale.
_Avoid_: HTML body, content
